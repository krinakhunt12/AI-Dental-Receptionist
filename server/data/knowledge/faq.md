# Frequently Asked Questions (sample data)

## Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are strongly recommended. Walk-ins are seen only if a slot is free.

## How do I reschedule or cancel?
Please tell us at least 4 hours before your appointment. You can ask the receptionist or call the clinic.

## Is there a cancellation fee?
There is no fee. Three consecutive no-shows may require a small booking deposit for future visits.

## Do you treat children?
Yes. Dr. Patel sees children for checkups, cleaning and fillings. Children under 12 must come with a parent or guardian.

## How long does a first visit take?
A first visit takes about 30 to 45 minutes, including a short registration and a checkup.

## Does teeth cleaning hurt?
Cleaning is usually painless. Some people with sensitive gums feel mild discomfort. The dentist can adjust the process.
