# Insurance and Payments (sample data)

## Payment methods
We accept cash, UPI, debit and credit cards. Payment is collected after the treatment.

## Insurance
We provide itemised bills and receipts that can be submitted for reimbursement to your insurer.
Cashless insurance is currently not available. Dental cover varies by plan, so please check your policy.

## Payment plans
Braces and implant treatments can be paid in instalments. The plan is arranged with the receptionist after the consultation.
