# Pre-Treatment Instructions (sample data)

## Before any visit
Brush and floss before you come. Bring any previous dental records or X-rays. Tell the dentist about your current medicines and allergies.

## Before a root canal or extraction
Eat a light meal before the appointment. Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before. Arrange for someone to accompany you if you prefer.

## Before teeth whitening
A cleaning is recommended at least a week before whitening. Avoid tea, coffee and tobacco on the day.

## After treatment
Avoid hot food and drinks until the numbness wears off. For any severe pain, swelling or bleeding after a treatment, call the clinic right away.
