# SmileCare Dental Clinic: Clinic Information (sample data)

Phone: +91 79 0000 0000. Email: hello@smilecare.example. WhatsApp support is planned.

## Opening hours
Monday to Friday: 10:00 AM to 7:00 PM (Dr. Patel). Saturday: 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM (Dr. Shah only). Sunday: closed.
The clinic is closed on major public holidays. Holiday closures are announced on the front desk and by phone.

## Parking and access
Free parking for about 15 cars is available in the basement. Two-wheeler parking is at the building entrance.
The clinic is on the first floor and is reachable by lift. The entrance is wheelchair accessible.

## Our dentists
Dr. Patel is our general dentist. He handles checkups, cleaning, fillings, root canals, whitening, extractions and dental implants. He works Monday to Friday, 10 AM to 7 PM.
Dr. Shah is our orthodontist. She handles braces, aligners and bite correction. She works Monday to Saturday, 11 AM to 6 PM.
