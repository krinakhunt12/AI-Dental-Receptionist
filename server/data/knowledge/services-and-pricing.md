# Dental Services and Pricing (sample data)

## Standard prices
General Checkup: Rs 500, 30 minutes.
Dental Cleaning (scaling and polishing): Rs 800, 30 minutes.
Dental Filling: Rs 1,500 per tooth, 45 minutes.
Root Canal Treatment: Rs 5,000, 90 minutes. A crown, if needed, is charged separately.
Teeth Whitening: Rs 3,000, 60 minutes.
Tooth Extraction: from Rs 1,000, 30 minutes. Surgical extraction of wisdom teeth is quoted after examination.

## Braces and implants
Braces: consultation Rs 500. Metal braces start from about Rs 35,000 for the full course. Ceramic braces and clear aligners cost more. The exact plan and cost are given after the consultation with Dr. Shah.
Dental Implants: consultation Rs 1,000. An implant starts from about Rs 25,000 per tooth. The final cost depends on the X-ray and bone assessment by Dr. Patel.

## What each treatment involves
Cleaning removes plaque and tartar and is recommended every 6 months.
Whitening is done in a single sitting. Mild tooth sensitivity for 1 to 2 days is common.
Root canal treatment removes infected pulp from the tooth and is usually completed in one or two visits.
